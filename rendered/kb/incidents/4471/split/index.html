The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July: a day and a month swapped. It reaches the desk from the app, by email and through booking-v2 forms. This is the bug #4471 describes.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Tenants on booking-v3 are day-first. Their people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The result looks the same as parser, a swap, but the parser fix does not touch it: the hint is wrong. Only reports sent through a booking-v3 form. Intake tags these by the reporter’s browser (en-US), which is a guess: it misses some and tags others that are not.

shift: the booking moves one day back. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May. The month stays. One meeting lands on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before the booking was made. That is a timezone problem, not a date-order one, and no date parser fix reaches it. The desk already noted these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The desk still counts them under parser, where they are filed by tenant, and so does this page; they are counted again here because they are a problem of their own.

Why one ticket hid three

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. Every subject here is some version of “the date is wrong”, so every report landed in one ticket. The subject tells you the symptom; the body, the tenant’s locale and its booking form tell you the cause. The table above counts the way the desk counts: causes.ts on every build, each report under the cause it is filed under, plus the desk’s one-day-move notes. So it stays right as more reports are merged.

What to do

  • Engineering: three fixes, three tickets. Ship the parser fix, then check the hint and shift rows still grow; they should, until their own fixes land.
  • Desk: until then, triage a new “wrong day” report by its body and tenant, not its subject. A swap through a booking-v3 form is hint; a one-day move is shift; any other swap from a day-first tenant is parser.
  • 0001 stays as it was accepted. Replacing it is a new decision record.
